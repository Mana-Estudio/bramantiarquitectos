## Is your project a good fit?

This service focuses on full apartment renovations in Madrid. To frame the first conversation we work with indicative investment bands of **€50,000 – €89,999** and **€90,000 – €119,999**. They are guidelines, not fixed prices or an estimate for your home: scope, size and finishes determine the proposal.

If you own a detached house, are planning a new build or your budget is in a different band, let us know and we will assess it specifically. Partial renovations are outside this service.

**Where we work:** Madrid (inside the M-30, Madrid Río, Concepción, Barrio del Pilar, La Elipa, El Carmen and Pinar de Chamartín), Pozuelo and Majadahonda. Other areas on request.

## Materials chosen with you

Choices start from use, maintenance, aesthetics and budget. We compare options, and every product and substitution is identified and agreed before it is installed.

## Handling the unexpected

A renovation can reveal conditions that were not visible at first. We analyse them, propose alternatives and document their impact on scope, cost and time before moving on.
