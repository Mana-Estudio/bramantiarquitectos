## Scope and key decisions

The intervention reorganises the layout and adapts the rooms to the household's needs. The interior design ties materials, lighting and furniture together for a continuous whole.

- **Layout:** study of needs, storage and the relationship between rooms.
- **Services and lighting:** building-services design and lighting calculated room by room.
- **Materials and furniture:** selection by space and design of specific pieces, such as the fireplace, the hall table and the kitchen.

## Preliminary design

Before defining the layout we study planning rules, building protection, singular elements and community rules, and compare options — including an open or semi-open kitchen.

## Result

The works follow the project, with site supervision and a final review of every detail. Installing the furniture completes the interior design.
