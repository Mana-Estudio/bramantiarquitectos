## The brief

The home needed a full renovation. The client wanted a functional, comfortable home for the family, with three bedrooms each with its own en-suite, a guest toilet, a utility room and an integrated kitchen-living space.

## Our proposal

We reconfigured the layout, adding a guest toilet and a dressing room in the main bedroom. Wasted corridor space was removed and used to enlarge the bedrooms and open up sight lines to the terrace, creating a larger kitchen-living room. An interior design project with characterful materials completed the work.

## Works and result

The works were complex: the building was in poor condition, with asbestos-cement downpipes, uneven floors and hidden defects. All of these issues were resolved within the renovation, with new building services and top-quality materials. The result is a carefully built home for a family to enjoy.
