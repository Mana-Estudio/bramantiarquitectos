## The origin

The brief was clear: keep the feeling of a classic home — its elegance and presence — and update it with a contemporary reading: more liveable, more comfortable, more in tune with life today.

The goal was not to "modernise". It was to refine.

## The proposal

The full renovation and interior design project updates the home while keeping its classic character, combining contemporary elements with a selection of textures and soft contrasts. Design decisions follow three principles: continuity between classic and contemporary, warm lighting, and materials that tie the rooms together.

## The atmosphere

- Effortless elegance
- Calm, warm light
- Spaces that invite you to stay
- A home that feels "right" from the moment you walk in

## The materials

- Noble textures, without excess
- Soft contrasts: matt and gloss, light and deep
- Discreet detail — the kind you discover rather than the kind that announces itself
- Materials chosen to last and to age well
