The project stands out for the careful restoration of original elements, such as the beams, preserved to bring a completely redesigned space to life. The result combines the authenticity of the past with contemporary function and style.

The layout was completely redesigned, enlarging the brief and adapting the spaces, with characterful materials, a full building-services design with lighting calculated for each room, and carefully selected furniture.

## Preliminary design

We study planning rules, building protection, singular elements and community rules, as well as the household's real needs: storage, lifestyle, an open or semi-open kitchen.

## Proposal

Based on the preliminary study, we propose layouts tailored to the owner, with materials for each room and a building-services calculation.

## Result

The works are carried out under thorough site supervision down to the final details, and the furniture is installed according to the interior design project.
