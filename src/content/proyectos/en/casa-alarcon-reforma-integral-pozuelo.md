A classic style renewed with modern touches. The home achieves an elegant, sophisticated look by combining contemporary designer furniture with classic elements such as mouldings. A light colour palette and carefully chosen furnishings enhance the brightness and sense of space.

## Preliminary design

We study planning rules, building protection, singular elements and community rules, as well as the household's real needs: storage, lifestyle, an open or semi-open kitchen.

## Proposal

Based on the preliminary study, we propose layouts tailored to the owner, with materials for each room and a building-services calculation.

## Result

The works are carried out under thorough site supervision down to the final details, and the furniture is installed according to the interior design project, leaving a personal home ready to move into.
