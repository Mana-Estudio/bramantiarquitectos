The project recovers the essence of original materials such as concrete and wood, and relies on characterful materials to organise the circulation by themselves. A clean architecture with timber slats forms a central volume in which every door is hidden and integrated, for a unique, minimalist interior.

The layout was completely redesigned, enlarging the brief and adapting the spaces. All building services were designed, with a lighting calculation for each room, and every piece of furniture was carefully chosen.

## Preliminary design

We study planning rules, building protection, singular elements and community rules, as well as the real needs of the household: storage, lifestyle, an open or semi-open kitchen.

## Proposal

Based on the preliminary study, we propose layouts tailored to the owner, with materials for each room and a building-services calculation.

## Result

Once the project is defined, the works are carried out under thorough site supervision down to the final snagging. Furniture is installed according to the interior design project, leaving a unique, personal home ready to move into.
