## The origin

Casa Werlyb was born from the need to turn a space into a home that is functional and in tune with the person who lives in it — a starting point based on listening and observation.

> "I need an open space that reflects my lifestyle."

## The idea

The intervention follows one clear idea: order the space to gain flow, light and balance. The architecture is pared back to the essentials so each room relates naturally to the whole.

## The atmosphere

The new layout opens up the home and creates a continuous connection between the social areas: a generous living and dining room with an integrated kitchen that flows straight onto a large terrace, designed as a flexible space for small gatherings, larger events and outdoor filming.

## Light and materials

Soft tones, restrained textures and precise junctions reinforce a feeling of calm. Lighting follows the new open layout and the glazed doors to the terrace, making the most of the visual connection with the outside.

## The setup

The recording setup is the operational core of the home, designed for a content creator. Its centrepiece is a single-piece floating desk, made to measure, that hides all the cabling and is lit from below for a feeling of lightness.

> "I wanted someone I could trust to help me make decisions."
